# Open decisions — Clarisa's call

Two decisions block real copy. Everything else can proceed without them.
They are hers, not ours. Recorded here so they get answered once instead of
being re-litigated every session.

---

## 1. Which headline is the site's?

Her own reference frames carry two, and they sell to different buyers.

| | **A — "Built to grow. Designed to convert."** | **B — "Digital stories in motion."** |
|---|---|---|
| Sub | Websites engineered to attract, convert, and scale your business. | Tech-luxury. Narrative focused. |
| Promise | The client's revenue | The studio's craft |
| Services shown | Design, development, SEO, hosting, automations, backend | Design · Motion · Strategy |
| Buyer | A Florida small-business owner who wants the phone to ring | A brand that already has budget and wants to look singular |
| Price posture | Packages, retainers | Project fees, selective engagements |
| Frames | `phase3-blueprint.png`, `phase5-final-demo.png` | `phase2-dissolve-digital-stories.jpeg`, `phase3-*` |

**They may not be alternatives.** With the newest opening frame in sequence, the
three headlines read as a progression — *Narrative assembled* → *Digital stories
in motion* → *Built to grow, designed to convert* — kinetic copy that replaces
itself as the phases advance and lands on the business promise. That is a real
option and arguably the strongest one.

**What we need:** which line is the visitor left sitting on when the motion stops.

---

## 2. Where does the animation end?

Phase 5 in the brief builds the blueprint into a finished **example client site**
branded "YOUR BUSINESS."

- **End on the demo site.** Strongest proof — the visitor watches a website get
  built and thinks *I want mine to look like that*. Cost: the last thing on screen
  is a fictional brand, not Zuniga.
- **End on Zuniga's own statement.** The demo becomes a beat inside the sequence
  rather than its destination, and the page settles on the studio.

**What we need:** one of the two. This determines the whole back half of the page.

---

## Not decisions — constraints already settled

- The demo client's metrics in her final frame (100+ launched, 2.4X conversion,
  90% retention, 24/7) are **invented furniture for a fictional brand**. They must
  be visibly framed as a demonstration. They can never read as Zuniga's numbers.
- The portfolio (PF Auto Style, Devise, American Pressure Clean, Justin Bartlett)
  is real work done under the Social Noodle name. Carrying it forward is honest.
  Implying Zuniga has operated for years is not.
- Voice: "I" for direct work, "Zuniga Studio" for institutional positions, "we"
  sparingly, never a fabricated team size. See `BLUEPRINT.md`.
