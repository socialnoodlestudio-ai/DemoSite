// 01 · Foundations · Palette

Matte ink, dusty rose, restrained gold.

Primary accent is metallic dusty rose. Rose-gold appears only at edges and highlights. Warm gold appears as luxury punctuation in less than 10% of any frame. The palette is engineered to feel cool, expensive, and architectural — never sweet or sentimental.
