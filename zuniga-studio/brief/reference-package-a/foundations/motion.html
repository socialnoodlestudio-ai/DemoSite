// 01 · Foundations · Motion

Slow, cinematic, intentional.

No bouncy. No snappy. Every transition uses one of three easings — chosen for emotional register, not novelty.

Easing tokens

Ease · Cinematic
cubic-bezier(0.22, 1, 0.36, 1)

For section reveals, scroll-tied parallax, navigation transitions. Decelerates gently.
Ease · Gravity
cubic-bezier(0.34, 0, 0.4, 1)

For particle attraction in Phase 1 — molecular pull-in feel.
Ease · Ink Drop
cubic-bezier(0.5, 0, 0.2, 1)

For typography emergence in Phase 4 — ink hitting paper.

Blueprint stabilizes. Liquid metal wave sweeps across, transforming wireframes into real components. Typography fills placeholders. Imagery develops. Micro-interactions activate.
18:00 +

Particles drift quietly. ZS monogram shimmer cycles every 20–30 seconds. Background geometry slowly breathes. Site is fully interactive.
